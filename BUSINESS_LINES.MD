# Business Lines

## Platform Foundation & Constraints

The products developed over the application must leverage the strengths of the server-side ML pipeline inference, combined with the portability of end-user visualization results on remote devices (phone, tablet, etc.).

This means user-facing services are subject to limitations related to:

- Connectivity availability
- Bugs and stability issues
- Data history problems over the web service

As a consequence, **internal development pipelines are asynchronous from the usability of the application** — they can evolve and improve independently of what the end user experiences at any given moment.

## Business Model

The initial strategic direction is to provide support for simple, repetitive tasks within **larger companies (B2B)**, taking advantage of the current ML pipeline capabilities.

---

## Business Niches

### 1. Textile Industry

**Segmentation and description of current stock and uncatalogued textile pieces.**

The system enables companies to identify, classify, and describe textile items that are not yet in their catalogue. If the application reaches sufficient user adoption around textile piece segmentation, the aggregated data can be used to:

- Identify current trends in textile usage
- Detect long-term trends in population-level consumption patterns

**Potential extension:** 3D rendering of uncatalogued pieces to generate 3D models. This extension is considered high complexity and results may not meet quality expectations at this stage.

---

### 2. Mechanical Pieces

**Segmentation and description of mechanical parts within engineering inventories.**

Engineering houses and industrial companies typically maintain very extensive part catalogues. The time cost of identifying an unknown piece — understanding what it is and where it belongs — can take several hours.

The system acts as:

- A **search engine** for known parts, enabling fast identification
- An **entry point** for cataloguing new or unidentified pieces

---

### 3. Plant Control

**Segmentation and counting of plant species over ecosystems.**

The system can recognize a specific type of plant, segment it, and count its occurrences within the field of view of a camera. This enables:

- Population counts of plant species by geographic region
- Monitoring of ecosystem evolution over time

The camera input can include satellite imagery systems. However, satellite-based data must be **carefully communicated** to end users, noting the inherent delays between measurement and actual ground status.

---

### 4. Gamification

**Segmentation and description of real-world objects by end users.**

Users interact with the system to segment objects in their everyday environment. In return, they receive **internal tokens** that can be exchanged for:

- Internal prizes
- Improved processing times
- Access to new models
- Interface customization options

This niche serves a dual purpose:

- **Short term:** Engages users and drives adoption of the platform
- **Long term:** Generates a foundational, globally distributed dataset that can be used to improve the core model and seed data pipelines for the other industry niches listed above

This is considered a primary source of initial research-grade data across all business lines.
